# Visual86 — provenance and scope

`maple_albedo86.png`, `maple_roughness86.png`, `maple_normal86.png` are procedurally authored 2048px maps, seed 86017, generated by `tools/Build-Visual86.py`. No external images are used. They may be distributed with this project.

`prototype_head.res`, `prototype_body.res`, `prototype_eyes.res`, `prototype_shoes.res` derive from the corresponding original meshes in `assets/active`. They retain the original bind coordinate frame, UVs and bone ordering, with PN-edge geometric refinement and interpolated normalized weights. The lower neck is fitted under the collar. They inherit the licensing requirements of the existing source meshes; they are not a new independently licensed human asset or a photogrammetric scan.

Only one athlete receives this prototype. High enables the refined head/eyes; Ultra adds body/shoes. All other athletes retain their original meshes. No new animation, rig, contact point or collider is authored.

The pilot also reuses the existing `T_Char_Head_Caucasian_M_01_*` texture set when its database appearance includes a beard, matching the existing M_01 body skin more closely. Clean-shaven and bald appearances retain the corresponding existing choices. These are existing licensed textures, not newly created scans; skin tint, hair and beard choices in the database are retained.

The roof panels, tubular truss diagonals and stair handrails are generated from native Godot geometry by `src/visual_graphics86.gd`, with no external model dependency.
